Builds bundle.zip for CodeDeploy, matching the layout every condor app's
appspec.yml expects.

condorZipCodeDeployBundle(
    jar: 'target/myapp.jar',          // built artifact
    appName: 'condor-myapp',           // bundle.zip -> <appName>.jar at the root
    serviceUnit: 'deploy/myapp.service', // systemd unit, copied under deploy/
)

Also copies scripts/start.sh, scripts/stop.sh, and appspec.yml from the repo
root - every condor app keeps those at fixed paths.
