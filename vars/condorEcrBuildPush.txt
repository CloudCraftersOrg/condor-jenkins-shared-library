Builds the Dockerfile in the workspace, pushes to ECR tagged with the
commit SHA, and sets env.IMAGE_DIGEST to the pushed digest.

condorEcrBuildPush(
    repository: 'condor-myapp',
    region: 'us-east-1', // optional, defaults to us-east-1
)

Downstream steps should deploy by digest (env.IMAGE_DIGEST), not the
mutable tag.
